# Proof status

The lattice-to-continuum construction in this working draft is conditional.
The opening section and the individual lemmas record the estimates still
needed for the selected family of Wilson lattice measures.

- Sections B and C still require uniform bounds for the actual differentiated
  response kernels and sector determinants, and an exact comparison of
  large-field masks and the fine-field domains on which Taylor estimates apply.
- Sections D and E give reductions for the cutoff comparison. The actual
  common domains, convergence on fixed inputs and uniform output-tail
  estimates still need proofs. The fixed-ratio coupling readout also depends
  on convergence of the incoming classical coefficients.
- Section E still requires bounds for the terms created by joining several
  insertions across scales, including the restart at the stopping level, and
  the guarded joint Gaussian comparison for the physical insertions.
- The short-distance trace coefficient in Section I additionally requires
  the quantitative transfer of the physical coefficient maps along the same
  scale history.

The independently reviewed local changes include the free two-form transfer,
the geometry used in the reflected finite-volume calculation and preservation
of the partition and marked integrals under normalized comparison kernels.

The full source archive was rebuilt in a fresh directory. The resulting PDF
had 306 pages. The distributed PDF was checked against the source manifest.
The review checked the local mathematical changes under their stated
hypotheses. Review is invited on the outstanding estimates and their
application to the construction.
