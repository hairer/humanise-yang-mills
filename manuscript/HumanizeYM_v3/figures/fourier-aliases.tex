  $p_\nu=z_\nu=0$ for $\nu=2,3,4$.

  left satisfy $3\theta_{z,1}\equiv p_1\pmod{2\pi}$.
  On the right their box multipliers are
  $(1+2\cos\theta_{z,1})/3$. As $p_1\to0$ the principal multiplier
  tends to one and both other multipliers vanish.}
