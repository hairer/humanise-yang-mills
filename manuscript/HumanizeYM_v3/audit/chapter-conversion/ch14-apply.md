Following the entries, the chapter now says "undecomposed" in the first case and "interacting" in the second. The author may prefer one word. Chapter 13 (input 18lp:8), which has the first use of sense 1, was being converted at the same time. Its word for "mixed" should be checked against "undecomposed" here.

- *Conserved line* proposes "the line `ker(div|_𝒦)`", and *physical line* proposes "the kernel of the divergence on `𝒦`". These are the same object. The words used here are "line of conserved tensors".
