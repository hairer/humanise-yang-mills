## First-use sentences added

None about Bałaban's construction: no table word with a difference to state occurs in this chapter. The two definitions the table asks for ("defined once") are:

- Line 482 (*separating family*): "A family of correlation functionals, each the correlation with fixed insertions at fixed points, is a \emph{separating family} for a space of fields if no nonzero field of that space lies in the kernel of all of them." The dictionary's meaning says "finite family"; "finite" was left out so that the sentence also covers Proposition 3:806, whose family (`𝒩_D`, all separated words) is not finite.
- Line 705 (*spectators*): "This holds for arbitrary finite Wick polynomials and arbitrary finite lists of \emph{spectators}, the remaining insertions $P_1,\ldots,P_n$ of the correlation." This is the first use in the book in this sense (01-lattice and 02-observables do not have the word; 02-equation-section has the other sense).

## Inconsistencies noticed

- *weight*: the entry (D, 2:50–51) proposes *scaling dimension*, but 02-observables.tex:50–51 still defines "weight" in the worktree at the time of writing (that file is being converted by another job). Chapter 3 no longer uses the word; its repetition of the definition at 168–169 was replaced by "As in Definition (ch02:labels), scaling dimensions add in products of these coordinates", which restates 2:49 and 3:96–97. If chapter 2 keeps "weight", nothing in chapter 3 depends on it.
- *free image*: the entry proposes *space of on-shell polynomials*; Appendix B proposes *on-shell polynomial space*. The second, which is the text's own (title 3:149, 3:588), was used.
- *separating family*: the entry says "finite family", but the family of Proposition 3:806 is not finite; the definition at 482 omits "finite". The entry's basis is R, yet the table asks for a definition, which had to be written (it follows the entry's *Meaning*).
- *flux* / *shell*: the proposals are descriptions of more than three words; the text's defined word *flux* was kept and "shell" removed at its only occurrence (546), before the shell is described at 672.
- *probe*: 751 ("jet probe") acts on curvature jets at `x_0`, while 939 defines `U_j` as functionals on scalar solution jets; one short term serves both, as the entry treats them as one sense.
- *weight operator*: the entry offers *generator of dilations* or *weighted Euler operator*; the first would make 689 read "for the dilation … this action is the generator of dilations", so the second was used.
