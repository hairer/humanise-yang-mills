# A generalized free field satisfies the public statement

**Result.** `theorem YangMills.jointExistence_of_freeField : YangMills.JointExistence` (`Main.lean`) is proved in Lean 4 from a scalar generalized free field. The import closure is the three public specification files (`PublicTheory.lean`, `PublicWilson.lean`, `ChallengeSpec.lean`), Mathlib, `../Adequacy.lean` and `../GroupIndependence.lean` (for `haarFamily` only). Neither `Solution`, `Construction` nor any other file of the upstream release is imported or copied. `#print axioms YangMills.jointExistence_of_freeField` gives `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`; there is no `sorry`, `axiom`, `native_decide`, `opaque`, `implemented_by` or `unsafe` in the development. This formalises finding M4 of the G01 review (issue #162).

**What it means.** The public statement `JointExistence` is satisfied, for every simple gauge group, by a theory with no Yang–Mills content: a generalized free field supplies the Wightman theory, the operator product expansion and the trace test, and the constant-insertion construction of `../Adequacy.lean` supplies the lattice clause. The gauge group enters only through the scalar that normalises the trace field to the required constant `3 dim 𝔤/π⁴`. This is a statement about what the public specification certifies. It shows no defect in the Lean proof of the upstream release and says nothing against its detailed theorem `YangMills.constructed`, whose conclusion concerns the Wilson lattice family itself.

**Construction** (`DESIGN.md`). One scalar field with one-particle measure `weight(p) dp`, `weight = (p²)²/log²(e+p²)` on the forward cone. Labels are the free vector space on multisets of derivative multi-indices (Wick monomials), filtered by the grade `Σ (4+|α|)`. The Hilbert space is the closed span of explicit word vectors inside an unsymmetrised direct sum of `L²` spaces (`Fock.lean`), and `Host.reconstruct` turns such a host into a `WightmanTheory` with `Axioms`. Euclidean correlations, their analytic continuation and the Wightman functions are explicit sums over matchings (`TwoPoint.lean`, `Wick.lean`). The operator product expansion uses one coefficient family built from partial matchings and Taylor expansion (`OPE.lean`). The trace field is a multiple of the basic field; its two-point function behaves like `384π · r⁻⁸ log⁻²(1/r)`, which gives the limit required by `TraceTest.shortDistance` and, by a general lemma, the exclusion of every finite Wick profile.

**Main intermediate results.** `Host.reconstruct`; `cut_identity` and `inner_Psi` (inner products of word vectors are Wightman functions); `Psi_covariant` (Poincaré covariance); `wightW_local` (locality for spacelike separated test functions, via the holomorphic function `SrC(√(|x|²−z²))`, flatness of the cross-correlation on the causal cone and dominated convergence); `Psi_faithful`; `charFun_diagMeasure`, `projOp_cone`, `transOp_invariant` (spectral representation, positive energy, unique vacuum); `contL_root`, `contL_boundary` (the Euclidean correlations are the continuation of the Wightman functions and have the right boundary values); `gope_coefficients`, `gope_remainder` (both OPE clauses); `Sr_asymptotic`, `wickProfile_excluded` (trace test).

**Layout** (`CONSOLIDATION.md`). `Labels`, `TwoPoint`, `Wick`, `Hierarchy`, `Fock`, `Symmetry`: definitions, unchanged since the reviews in `reviews/`. `Analytic/`, `Reconstruction/`, `Theory/`, `Continuation/`, `OPE/`, `Proofs/`: one module per lemma or lemma group in dependency order; `Analytic.lean`, `Theory.lean` and the others re-export them. `Main.lean`: the assembly, unchanged since phase 1. `AxiomReport.lean`: prints the axioms of the main theorem and of the 75 lemmas of `TASKS.md` and fails the build unless each of the 81 audited declarations depends on exactly the three standard axioms. `FinalCheck.lean`: restates the conclusion against `JointExistence` and against its unfolded body. `Check/check_conventions.py`: numeric tests of the conventions. `reviews/`: four GPT-6 Astra reviews of design and statements, made before proof work. 72 Lean files, about 18,000 lines.
